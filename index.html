Knowledge base

Searchable FAQ & answers

Type any keyword — training, EPF, pricing, licensing — and the list filters as you type.

What industries do you typically serve?
We work with SMEs and mid-market companies across healthcare, manufacturing, retail, F&B and professional services. Each engagement is tailored — no two clients get the same playbook.
How long does a typical corporate training program take?
Most programs run 6–12 weeks with weekly sessions. Short executive workshops are 2–3 days. We always propose the shortest path to a measurable outcome.
Do you help with clinic licensing and accreditation?
Yes. Our medical consulting team supports end-to-end clinic set-up, including facility layout, regulatory documentation, SOP design and accreditation readiness.
What does your safety consulting cover?
Risk assessments, OSH policy drafting, incident investigation, on-site audits, and employee safety training — aligned to local OSH regulations.
How is pricing structured?
Project-based for consulting, retainer for ongoing marketing. Training is priced per cohort. You'll get a fixed-fee proposal after a discovery call — no hourly billing.
Can you handle packaging sourcing overseas?
Yes. We coordinate with vetted vendors in China, Vietnam and Malaysia for structural design, material sourcing, QA and shipping — all with a single point of contact.
How quickly can we get started?
Typically within 1–2 weeks of contract signing. Discovery calls can usually be scheduled within 48 hours.
Do you provide compliance documentation for audits?
Yes. We prepare full audit-ready documentation including SOPs, training records, risk registers and corrective action logs for both safety and medical engagements.
